# AssetVault early tester script

Use this with a friend, family member, renter, contractor, or small-business owner who has not seen AssetVault before. Ask them to share their screen or record the session. Do not explain where to click unless they are truly stuck.

## Opening prompt

> “Please try this as if you wanted to make sure you could prove what you own after a theft, disaster, or insurance claim. Say out loud what you expect each screen to do. There are no wrong answers; we are testing the product, not you.”

## Tasks

1. Open the interactive demo without creating an account.
2. Choose the use case and concern that best fit you.
3. Tell us what you think the Top 10 Assets Challenge wants you to do.
4. Add one important item using only the information you would normally have on hand.
5. Find the item’s Proof Score and explain what you think is still missing.
6. Try to estimate a replacement value while on Proof Check. Describe what the upgrade message means to you.
7. Find the Incident Packet option. Would you know when to use it?
8. Find the plan that you believe fits you. Explain why.

## Questions after each task

- What did you expect to happen?
- What, if anything, was confusing?
- Was any wording unclear, alarming, or overly technical?
- Where did you hesitate or feel like you might lose your work?

## Five-minute wrap-up

Ask for a 1–5 rating and one sentence for each:

- I understand what AssetVault does.
- I know what I should document first.
- I would trust myself to use this after a loss.
- I understand what I get free versus with Complete.
- I would be likely to use or recommend this.

Record the tester’s use case, the first point of confusion, the task that felt most valuable, and whether they would use the product at the stated price. Do not collect their actual serial numbers, receipts, or sensitive photos during this early feedback session.
